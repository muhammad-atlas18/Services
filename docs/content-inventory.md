## Content guardrails

- Do not claim city-wide Lahore coverage until exact operational areas are confirmed.
- Do not publish prices, starting prices, price ranges, or price-related schema.
- Final quotation always depends on site condition, materials, equipment compatibility and complete work scope.
- Use only substantiated experience, warranty, safety and response-time claims.
- Use original/approved imagery and verified reviews only.
